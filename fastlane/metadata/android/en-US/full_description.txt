Kanji Jojo is the community continuation of Kanji Dojo, the original app by Yaroslav Shuliak. Following the original project's archival, this independent open-source fork continues development under the GNU GPL v3.0 license, with new features and more improvements to come.

Learn and practice writing Japanese characters

Features
- Study kana (both Hiragana and Katakana) and kanji
- Practice by following JLPT levels or school grades
- Create your own list to study, more than 6000 characters in total are available
- Works offline
- Write personal notes of up to 500 characters for each kana or kanji, shared across decks
- Read and edit notes on character info pages and during practice
- Reveal notes as a hint before answering, or read them after revealing the answer
- Notes autosave and are included in local backups and sync

Moving from Kanji Dojo
1. In Kanji Dojo, open Settings > Backup & Restore > Create backup. Keep the exported ZIP outside the app.
2. In Kanji Jojo, open Settings > Backup & Restore > Restore from backup and select the ZIP. Restoring replaces any existing Kanji Jojo study data; export it first if you need to keep it.
3. Check your decks and review progress, then sign in from Kanji Jojo and sync the imported local data.

Old accounts and cloud backups do not transfer automatically. Signing in on the website alone does not import or upload study data. Offline study and backup/restore do not require an account.

Migration guide: https://kanji-jojo.com/migration
