Kanji Jojo continues the archived Kanji Dojo project, with more improvements to come.
- My notes: 500-character notes per character, shared across decks, in info pages and practice.
- Reveal notes as a practice hint; autosave, backup, and sync included.
- Kanji Jojo account sign-in and sync.
Moving from Kanji Dojo? Export a backup there, restore it in Kanji Jojo, check your progress, then sync the imported local data. Old cloud backups do not move automatically.
