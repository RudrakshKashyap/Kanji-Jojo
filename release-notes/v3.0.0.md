# Kanji Jojo 3.0.0

Kanji Jojo is the community continuation of [Kanji Dojo](https://github.com/syt0r/Kanji-Dojo), originally created by Yaroslav Shuliak. After the original repository was archived, this independent fork became a new home for continued development under the same GNU GPL v3.0 license.

This release brings My notes and Kanji Jojo's own account and sync service. More features and improvements are planned, and ideas, bug reports, and contributions are welcome.

## My notes

- Keep up to 500 characters of personal notes for each kana or kanji, shared across decks.
- Read and edit notes on character info pages and during reading and writing practice.
- On the practice question, choose Reveal to see your notes as a hint. Notes also appear when the answer is revealed.
- Use the compact notes card with an edit action, character counter, and Done button. Tapping outside the editor dismisses focus and the keyboard.
- Notes autosave locally and are included in your study-data backups and sync.

## Moving from Kanji Dojo

**Export → import → sync, in that order.**

1. In **Kanji Dojo**, open **Settings → Backup & Restore → Create backup**. Keep the exported ZIP outside the app until you have verified the migration.
2. Install **Kanji Jojo 3.0.0 or newer** from this repository's release assets.
3. In **Kanji Jojo**, open **Settings → Backup & Restore → Restore from backup**, choose the ZIP, and confirm Restore. Restoring replaces any study data already in Kanji Jojo; export that data first if you need to keep it.
4. Check that your decks and review progress were restored.
5. Then sign in from **Kanji Jojo** and sync the imported data. If a conflict appears, choose the imported **local data** to upload.

Old Kanji Dojo accounts and cloud backups do not transfer automatically. Signing in on the website alone does not import or upload study data. Offline study and local backup/restore do not require an account.

See the [migration guide](https://kanji-jojo.com/migration), [project README](https://github.com/RudrakshKashyap/Kanji-Jojo#readme), and [community Discord](https://discord.gg/ptAJzGnHYk).
